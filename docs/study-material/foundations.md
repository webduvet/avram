# Foundations — central bank / payment systems

- [BIS CPMI Red Book statistics methodology (PDF)](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/publ/d168.pdf) — **free** — Official methodology for Red Book payment/FMI statistics (definitions of LVPS, RPS, FPS, clearing/settlement concepts).
- [BIS Red Book methodology landing page](https://www.bis.org/publications/methodology-statistics-payments-and-financial-market-infrastructures-cpmi-countries-red-book-statistics) — **free** — Narrative intro to the revised Red Book methodology and how country vs comparative tables work.
- [BIS Data Portal — CPMI FMI / Red Book](https://data.bis.org/topics/CPMI_FMI) — **free** — Live Red Book tables for payment systems, CSDs, CCPs across CPMI jurisdictions.
- [ECB — What is T2?](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/t2/html/index.en.html) — **free** — Primer on Europe’s RTGS (replaced TARGET2 in 2023); ISO 20022, CLM+RTGS, central-bank money finality.
- [ECB — What is TIPS?](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/tips/html/index.en.html) — **free** — Instant settlement in central bank money 24/7; SCT Inst / multi-currency context for Sim Lab “instant rail” models.
- [Federal Reserve — Fedwire Funds Services](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fedfunds_about.htm) — **free** — Official RTGS primer: immediate, final, irrevocable large-value transfers.
- [Fedwire Funds Service product sheet (PDF)](https://www.frbservices.org/binaries/content/assets/crsocms/financial-services/wires/funds.pdf) — **free** — Short FRBServices product overview of Fedwire Funds use cases and flow.
- [Federal Reserve — Automated Clearinghouse Services](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fedach_about.htm) — **free** — FedACH vs EPN dual-operator ACH model; batch credit/debit; Same Day ACH context.
- [Federal Reserve — FedNow Service](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fednow_about.htm) — **free** — Official instant-payment service overview (live since Jul 2023).
- [FedNow Technical Overview and Planning Guide (PDF)](https://explore.fednow.org/resources/technical-overview-guide.pdf) — **free** — Best public technical primer: pacs.008 flow, MQ, ISO 20022, 20s timeout, settlement steps.
