# Courses

- [Coursera — Analyze Payment & Settlement Systems in Banking (EDUCBA)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/analyze-payment-settlement-systems-banking) — **audit / Free Trial / paid certificate** — End-to-end payment lifecycle, ACH/wire/card, clearing & settlement; look for “Full Course, No Certificate” / audit where offered.
- [edX — How to audit a class](https://www.edx.org/resources/how-to-audit-an-edx-class) — **free guide** — How to access edX materials without paying for verified track (search “payment systems” / “banking” courses and audit).
- [SWIFT Smart](https://www.swift.com/myswift/services/training/swiftsmart) — **free only with institutional SWIFT access** — Best structured ISO 20022 / payments modules if the institution has a BIC login; instructor-led extras are paid.
- [SWIFT — ISO 20022 for Dummies ebook](https://www.swift.com/campaign/iso-20022/iso-20022-dummies) — **free (reg)** — Lightweight “course substitute” before diving into catalogues.
- [Payments Academy — Certification (CFTPM)](https://paymentsacademy.net/certification/) — **courses free; exam enrollment required; $49/yr recert after pass** — Ecosystem / Economics / Building Blocks / Compliance tracks — reasonably priced for industry CE.
- [Payments Academy — home](https://paymentsacademy.net/) — **mostly free course access** — Broader prepaid/fintech payments CE library.

*PCN:* No clear, verified public “PCN” payments academy/training product with transparent free/cheap pricing was found in this pass (avoid inventing). Prefer Payments Academy + Coursera/edX audit + SWIFT Smart if available.

*University OCW:* No strong dedicated “payment systems” university open course with verified free materials surfaced beyond general finance audits (Yale Financial Markets etc. are tangential — omitted to stay on-brief).
